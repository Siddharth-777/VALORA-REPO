What is VALORA?

VALORA is an experimental economic sandbox where AI agents—consumers, firms, banks, and regulators—interact autonomously within a simulated economy. Watch cycles unfold, test policy interventions, and analyze outcomes through intelligent agent workflows.

Dynamic Economic Cycles

Simulate realistic business cycles from expansion through peak, recession, and trough. Track GDP growth, inflation rates, unemployment levels, and consumer confidence in real-time.

Intelligent AI Agents

Autonomous agents make decisions using reinforcement learning and heuristics. Consumers optimize spending, firms adjust pricing, banks evaluate loans, and regulators balance fiscal policy.

Policy Analysis Crew

Five specialized AI agents collaborate to evaluate policy proposals: economic analysts assess context, tax advisors recommend adjustments, and critics provide balanced perspectives.

Shock Scenarios

Test economic resilience by introducing real-world shocks—financial crises, pandemics, supply disruptions—and observe how agents adapt their strategies and the economy recovers.

Blockchain Logging

Transparent transaction ledger using blockchain-style hashing. Every economic action is recorded, hashed, and chained for full auditability and replay capabilities.

Interactive Simulation

Visualize actor behaviors through dynamic charts and network graphs. Adjust policy parameters, run time-stepped simulations, and compare alternative economic trajectories.
